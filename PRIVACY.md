# Privacy Policy for AdPilot

**Last Updated:** August 2026

AdPilot is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
AdPilot does **not** collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data, search history, browsing activity, or IP addresses.

## 2. Local Processing
All core functions and logic run strictly within your local browser on your personal device. No user data is ever sent to external servers or third-party analytics services.

## 3. Minimal Permissions
The permissions requested in `manifest.json` are strictly required for the core functionality of the extension and are not used to gather user data.

## 4. Contact & Support
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/CCMhQgvFS).
